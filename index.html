Find your NOC

Search by job title, a keyword from your duties, or a 5-digit code. Then compare duties: IRCC assigns your NOC by what you actually do, not your job title.

How to confirm your NOC

  1. Write down the 5–8 tasks you spend most of your week on, with rough percentages.
  2. Search above for your title and two or three of those tasks. Shortlist every code that comes up.
  3. For each shortlisted code, check that the description matches your work and that you do most of its main duties.
  4. Check the TEER (second digit). Express Entry counts only TEER 0, 1, 2 and 3 as skilled work.
  5. Ask your employer for a reference letter listing your real duties, hours, dates and pay. It must support the code you choose.

Commonly confused occupations

The same job title can sit in two codes. The difference is in the duties, and it can change your high-wage points.
